The continent of Suboria lies south of the mainland and can only be reached by ship. It is believed to be a great archipelago, with its southern portions joined together by vast sheets of ice. The eastern half of Suboria is rumored to hold the Yuki Province, the seat of the fabled Dragon Emperor. Western Suboria is home to various large human cities as well settlements of drow, gnolls and giants. The boldest explorers travel south across the eastern Ice Lands to find frozen mountains stalked by predators and undead.

The Demon Realms
